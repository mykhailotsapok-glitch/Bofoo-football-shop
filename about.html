WELCOME TO BOFOO

About Bofoo

Bofoo is a football-inspired store concept built for fans of boots, kits, teamwear and the latest football style.

Our Story

Bofoo was created as a modern football store concept that brings together the energy of the game with stylish and easy-to-use web design. The idea behind Bofoo is simple: create a place where football fans can explore boots, clothing and the latest trends in one clean experience.

From speed boots to retro kits, Bofoo is designed to feel exciting, modern and full of football identity.

MY WEBSITE JOURNEY

The Story Behind Bofoo

How I created a football-inspired website for my BTEC I.T. project.

Why I Created Bofoo

Bofoo is a football-inspired website that I created for my BTEC I.T. project. I wanted to design a website that connects my interest in football with the web development skills I am learning.

My aim was to create something modern, bold and visually interesting, like real football shopping websites.

My Idea

I wanted to create a football website that felt modern, stylish and exciting.

My Design

I used dark colours, lime highlights, banners, cards and product sections inspired by sports websites.

What I Learned

This project helped me improve my HTML, CSS, layout design and file organisation skills.
